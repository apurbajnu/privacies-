Privacy Policy for QuizClimb

Last Updated: January 10, 2026

Important: QuizClimb is designed with your privacy in mind. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

QuizClimb collects minimal information to provide you with the best gaming experience:

  • Player Name: A nickname you choose for multiplayer games (stored locally on your device only)
  • Player Age: Your age helps us show age-appropriate questions (stored locally on your device only)
  • Game Progress & Scores: Your gameplay history, achievements, and high scores (stored locally on your device)
  • Camera Access: Only used to scan QR codes for joining local multiplayer games

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Displaying your name during multiplayer games
  • Showing age-appropriate quiz questions
  • Saving your game progress and scores
  • Scanning QR codes to join local WiFi games

We do NOT:

  • Create user accounts or profiles
  • Require login or registration
  • Store data on cloud servers
  • Sell or share your data with third parties
  • Show advertisements
  • Use analytics or tracking services

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Local Storage Only: All your data (name, age, progress, scores) is stored locally on your device
  • No Servers: QuizClimb does NOT use any external servers. Your data never leaves your device
  • Multiplayer: Local WiFi multiplayer works on the same network only using UDP protocol. No internet connection is required, and no data is transmitted over the internet
  • Camera Images: When scanning QR codes, images are processed in real-time and are NOT stored or saved
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the app or using the "Reset Progress" option in the app settings

4. Permissions Required

QuizClimb requests the following permissions:

  • Camera: Optional permission used only for scanning QR codes to join local multiplayer games. You can still join games manually without granting camera permission.
  • Local Network Access: Required for WiFi multiplayer feature so devices on the same network can discover and connect to each other.

5. Children's Privacy

QuizClimb is suitable for users of all ages, including children. We are committed to protecting children's privacy:

  • We do NOT collect any personal information from children
  • We do NOT require any parental consent for using the app
  • No data is transmitted externally
  • All data remains on the device

6. Third-Party Services

QuizClimb does NOT use any third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integration
  • Cloud storage services
  • Crash reporting services

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained on your device until you:

  • Delete the app from your device
  • Use the "Reset Progress" option in app settings
  • Clear the app's data through your device settings

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Providing a notice within the app (for major changes)

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored by the app on your device
  • Delete your data at any time
  • Opt-out of camera permission (multiplayer will still work via manual connection)
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data
